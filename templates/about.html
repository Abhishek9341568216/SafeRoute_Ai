About & Academic Methodology

Comprehensive system architecture, dataset descriptions, mathematical formulations, and evaluation design.

Project Objectives & Scope

SafeRoute AI is an end-to-end artificial intelligence decision-support platform designed to address urban traffic congestion and accident risk prediction. Rather than claiming autonomous prevention, the platform provides data-driven risk scoring, congestion forecasts, and spatial hotspot detection to support preventive urban traffic management.

Complementary Datasets

1. UCI Metro Interstate Traffic Volume Dataset

Target: traffic_volume (Continuous Regression)

Contains 48,204 hourly traffic readings on Interstate 94 in Minneapolis, MN (2012–2018), accompanied by temperature, rain, snow, cloud cover, and categorical weather states.

2. US Accidents Dataset (2016–2023)

Target: Severity / high_risk_target (Classification & Clustering)

Countrywide traffic accident records covering spatial coordinates (Latitude, Longitude), environmental weather parameters, and roadway infrastructure flags (Traffic Signals, Crossings, Junctions).

Machine Learning Pipelines

  • Traffic Volume Regression: Compared Linear Regression, Polynomial Regression (Degree 2), Decision Tree, Random Forest, and Gradient Boosting Regressors. Evaluated via MAE, MSE, RMSE, and R² metrics.
  • Accident Risk Classification: Constructed binary risk target (Severity ≥ 3 mapped to High Risk). Evaluated Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, Random Forests, Support Vector Machines (SVM), and K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) using Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC, and Confusion Matrices.
  • Spatial Clustering: Evaluated K-Means (k=8) centroid partitioning against DBSCAN (eps=0.3, min_samples=15) density-based clustering to discover geographic accident hotspots while filtering spatial noise points (-1).

System Framing & Limitations

Decision-Support Disclaimer & Scope

SafeRoute AI does not assert direct accident prevention or live vehicular remote control. Predictions reflect historical statistical correlations between environmental features, temporal attributes, and past traffic incidents. The system serves as an analytical decision-support tool for traffic safety authorities and urban planners.
